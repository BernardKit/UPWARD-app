UPWARD Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24/11/26

In short: everything you enter in UPWARD stays on your phone. There is no account and no UPWARD server. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data, with your consent where required. Premium removes ads.

UPWARD (“the app”) is a habit and self-improvement tracker for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles. Contact: bernardkitney.work@gmail.com".

Data that stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only on your device. That includes habits and completion history, tasks, schedule entries, books and reading progress, study and exercise sessions, goals, notes, your optional first name, reminder times and settings such as your theme. There is no UPWARD server and no account, and we never receive, see or sell this data.

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data. You can export a backup file from Settings. That file is saved wherever you choose and stays under your control.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location
  • information about your interactions with ads

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your permission first. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from partner apps.

Premium subscribers see no ads, and the app does not request ads for them.

Purchases

Google Play processes all subscriptions. The app only learns whether your subscription is active and never sees your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play → Subscriptions.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Children

UPWARD is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if the app's data practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
